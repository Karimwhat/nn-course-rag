# Differentiating the Softmax Activation
*February 2, 2026 | 8:32 AM*

Assume the outputs from last layer are $\hat{y}$ then softmax activation:
$$\hat{p}_i = \frac{e^{\hat{y}_i}}{\sum_j e^{\hat{y}_j}}$$

$L = -\sum_i y_i \log \hat{p}_i$

$$\frac{\partial \hat{p}_i}{\partial \hat{y}_k} = \begin{cases} \hat{p}_k \cdot (1 - \hat{p}_k) & \text{if } i = k \\ -\hat{p}_k \hat{p}_i & \text{if } i \neq k \end{cases}$$

$$\frac{\partial L}{\partial \hat{y}_k} = -\sum_i y_i \frac{\partial}{\partial \hat{y}_k} \log \hat{p}_i$$
$$= -\sum_i y_i \frac{1}{\hat{p}_i} \frac{\partial \hat{p}_i}{\partial \hat{y}_k}$$
$$= -\sum_{i=k} y_i (1 - \hat{p}_k) + \sum_{i \neq k} y_i \hat{p}_k$$

$$= -y_k + \sum_i y_i \hat{p}_k$$
$$= -y_k + \hat{p}_k \sum_i y_i$$
$$\frac{\partial L}{\partial \hat{y}_k} = \hat{p}_k - y_k$$

*   $\hat{p}_k$: prob after softmax
*   $\hat{y}_k$: output before softmax
*   $y_k$: ground-truth indicator

### Example
Use training example:
Input: $\vec{x} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix} \quad$ Target: $z = 3$

Weights: initially all are 0.5.
Activation: ReLU.
Loss: Sum of squares error
$$L = \frac{1}{2} \sum \sum (z_k - \hat{z}_k)^2$$

---

Forward:
$r_1 = v_{0,1} x_0 + v_{1,1} x_1 + v_{2,1} x_2 \quad y_1 = 2$
$= 2$
$r_2 = 2 \quad y_2 = 2$

$s_1 = w_0 y_0 + w_1 y_1 + w_2 y_2 \quad \hat{z}_1 = 2.5$
$= 2.5$

Loss:
$$L = \frac{1}{2} \sum \sum (z_k - \hat{z}_k)^2$$
$$= \frac{1}{2} (2_1 - \hat{z}_1)^2$$
$= 1/8 = 0.125$

Backpropagation:
$$\frac{\partial L}{\partial w_j} = \frac{\partial L}{\partial s_k} \frac{\partial s_k}{\partial w_j}$$
$$\frac{\partial L}{\partial s_k} = \frac{\partial L}{\partial \hat{z}_k} a'(s_k) \quad \frac{\partial s_k}{\partial w_0} = 1$$

---

$$\frac{\partial L}{\partial s_k} = (\hat{z}_k - z_k) \cdot a'(s_k)$$
$$= (2.5 - 3) \cdot 1$$
$= -0.5$

$\frac{\partial s_k}{\partial w_0} = 1, \frac{\partial s_k}{\partial w_1} = 2, \frac{\partial s_k}{\partial w_2} = 2$
$\frac{\partial L}{\partial w_0} = -0.5, \frac{\partial L}{\partial w_1} = -1, \frac{\partial L}{\partial w_2} = -1$

$\nabla L(\vec{w}) = [-0.5, -1, -1]$

$$\frac{\partial L}{\partial v_{i,j}} = \frac{\partial L}{\partial r_j} \frac{\partial r_j}{\partial v_{i,j}}$$
$$\frac{\partial L}{\partial r_j} = \sum_k \frac{\partial L}{\partial s_k} w_j \cdot a'(r_j)$$
$$= -0.5 \cdot w_j \cdot a'(r_j)$$
$$\frac{\partial L}{\partial r_1} = -0.5 \cdot 0.5 \cdot 1 = -0.25$$

$\frac{\partial L}{\partial r_2} = -0.5 \cdot 0.5 \cdot 1 = -0.25$

$\frac{\partial L}{\partial v_{0,1}} = \frac{\partial L}{\partial r_1} \cdot x_0 = -0.25 \quad \frac{\partial L}{\partial v_{0,2}} = -0.25$
$\frac{\partial L}{\partial v_{1,1}} = \frac{\partial L}{\partial r_1} \cdot x_1 = -0.25 \quad \frac{\partial L}{\partial v_{1,2}} = -0.25$
$\frac{\partial L}{\partial v_{2,1}} = \frac{\partial L}{\partial r_1} \cdot x_2 = -0.5 \quad \frac{\partial L}{\partial v_{2,2}} = -0.5$

$\nabla L(\vec{V}) = [-0.25, -0.25]$

Update with gradient descent: $\alpha = 0.1$
$w_0 \leftarrow w_0 - \alpha \cdot \frac{\partial L}{\partial w_0}$
$\leftarrow 0.5 - 0.1 \cdot (-0.5)$
$\leftarrow 0.55$
$w_1 \leftarrow 0.5 - 0.1 \cdot (-1)$
$\leftarrow 0.6$

$\leftarrow 0.5 - 0.1 \cdot (-0.25)$

### Unsupervised Learning in Neural Networks
### Self-Organizing Maps (Kohonen Networks)
Consider neurons in the map to be prototypes
1. Given input $x$, compute most similar neuron $A$.
2. Adjust $A$ to make it more similar to $x$.
3. Adjust neighbours of $A$ to be more similar to $x$ (to some lesser extent).
4. Repeat.
