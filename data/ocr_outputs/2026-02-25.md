### Backprop on Convolution
$$w_i \leftarrow w_i - \alpha \frac{\partial L}{\partial w_i}$$

$w_i$ - weights in the kernel.
Output of convolution is
$$O_t = (x * w)(t) = \sum_{a = -\infty}^{\infty} x(t+a) w(a)$$

For a fixed width kernel of width $k$
$$O_t = w_{-\frac{k-1}{2}} x_{t-\frac{k-1}{2}} + \dots + w_0 x_t + \dots + w_{\frac{k-1}{2}} x_{t+\frac{k-1}{2}}$$

COMP 4107 W2026 Page 2

---

If $k = 3$:
$$O_t = w_{-1} x_{t-1} + w_0 x_t + w_1 x_{t+1}$$

$$\frac{\partial L}{\partial w_i} = \sum_t \left( \frac{\partial L}{\partial O_t} \right) \left( \frac{\partial O_t}{\partial w_i} \right)$$

*   $\frac{\partial L}{\partial O_t}$ is given by subsequent layer
*   $\frac{\partial O_t}{\partial w_i} = x_{t+i}$

To facilitate backprop:
$$\frac{\partial L}{\partial x_i} = \sum_t \left( \frac{\partial L}{\partial O_t} \right) \left( \frac{\partial O_t}{\partial x_i} \right)$$

*   $\frac{\partial L}{\partial O_t}$ is given by subsequent layer
*   $\frac{\partial O_t}{\partial x_i} = w_{i-t}$

This does not handle long-term dependencies well.

$$h_t \approx W h_{t-1} \approx W^2 h_{t-2} \approx \dots \approx W^t h_0$$

If compute eigendecomposition of $W = Q D Q^T$:
$$h_t \approx (Q D Q^T)^t h_0$$
$$h_t \approx Q D^t Q^T h_0$$

...aligned with eigenvectors of $W$
