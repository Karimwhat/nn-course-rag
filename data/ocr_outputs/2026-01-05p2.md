**TIPS FOR THE QUIZZES**
* Start early
* Do some of the exercises in a "quiz-like" environment
* You will not be able to look up everything during the quiz
* Prepare a review sheet with the most important material on it

*January 5, 2026 | Matthew S. Holden, School of Computer Science, Carleton University | 6*

---

**TIPS FOR THE ASSIGNMENTS**
* Start early
* Work in groups
* Read the entire assignment
* Understand the material before starting
* Follow the specifications for the implementations
* Do not plan on using the extension
* Upload drafts periodically

*January 5, 2026 | Matthew S. Holden, School of Computer Science, Carleton University | 7*

**TIPS FOR THE PROJECT**
* Start early
* Submit your proposal early
* Provide lots of details in the proposal
* Set weekly goals for what you want to accomplish
* Do something novel
* Read about related work before starting
* Do not plan on using the extension
