$Q(s, a) = \Theta_1 f_1(s, a) + \Theta_2 f_2(s, a)$  
$f_1(s, a) = \text{\# of clean squares}$  
$f_2(s, a) = \text{vacuum in dirty square } \Leftrightarrow \text{ a is Clean}$

$\gamma = 0.9$

2. $Q(s_1, a_1) = 1 \cdot 3 + 1 \cdot 1 = 4$

$Q(s_2, L) = 1 \cdot 4 + 1 \cdot 1 = 5$  
$Q(s_2, R) = 1 \cdot 4 + 1 \cdot 1 = 5$  
$Q(s_2, C) = 1 \cdot 4 + 1 \cdot 0 = 4$

3. $L = \frac{1}{2} (Q(s_1, a_1) - Y)^2$  
$Y = r(s_1, a_1) + \gamma \max_{a'} Q(s_2, a')$  
$Y = 3 + 0.9 \cdot 5 = 7.5$  
$L = \frac{1}{2} (4 - 7.5)^2$

$\frac{\partial L}{\partial \Theta_i} = (Q(s_1, a_1) - Y) \cdot \frac{\partial}{\partial \Theta_i} Q(s_1, a_1)$

For the output layer:  
$\frac{\partial Q}{\partial \Theta_i} = \text{ReLU}' \left( \sum w_i y_i \right) \cdot y_i$  
$y_i$ is the inputs to the output layer  
$w_i$ is the weights for the output layer  
$Q = \text{ReLU} \left( \sum w_i y_i \right)$  
$\frac{\partial Q}{\partial w_1} = \text{ReLU}'(\sum w_i y_i) \cdot y_1 = 7$

$\frac{\partial L}{\partial w_1} = (7 - 10.2) \cdot 7 = -22.4$
